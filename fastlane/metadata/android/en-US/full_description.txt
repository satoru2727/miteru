Miteru is an unofficial Android client for your self-hosted KonomiTV server.

LIVE TV
Tap a channel to start watching. Each card shows the current and next programme, viewer count and how far the broadcast has progressed. Search channels and filter by terrestrial, BS or CS.

PROGRAM GUIDE
Scroll a 24-hour grid with a fixed channel column. Switch between today, tomorrow and the day after, zoom the time scale, jump back to the current time, and open a programme for its description, genres and reservation status.

RECORDING RESERVATIONS (EDCB BACKEND)
Add a reservation from a programme's detail sheet, then manage it in the Reservations tab: enable, disable, or cancel. Requires a KonomiTV server using the EDCB backend.

NICONICO LIVE COMMENTS
Overlay NX-Jikkyo comments while watching live TV, and past comments while playing a recording. Can be turned off in Settings.

RECORDINGS
Browse recordings with thumbnails, search by title, filter MPEG-TS or MP4, sort by date, and resume from where you stopped.

PLAYER
Quality switching, primary and secondary audio, picture-in-picture, background playback with a media notification, double-tap to seek 10 seconds, and channel zapping while watching live TV.

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only network destination is the KonomiTV server you configure.

KonomiTV is a project by tsukumijima. Miteru is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with the upstream project.
